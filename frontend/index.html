AURA-FLOOD CIVIC HYDROLOGICAL DATASET

Dataset Purpose & Early-Warning Architecture

🎯 Why this dataset was developed:

Traditional meteorological forecasts (IMD / numerical models) predict rainfall across a large district (e.g., "50mm in Mumbai or Delhi"), but citizens, emergency responders, and commuters face acute micro-local hazards: Which underpass is underwater? Which saucer basin will flood first? Where are the chokepoints? AURA was developed to bridge this critical "last-mile" urban flood vulnerability gap across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories of India.

Comprehensive Nationwide Dataset Components

🏛️
82+ Micro-Localities Across 36 States & UTs

Every Indian state and UT is documented with elevation profiles, natural saucer depression basins, and drainage outfall constraints (such as coastal high-tide sea locks and river backflows).

🚧
50+ Chronic Urban Chokepoints

Catalog of critical underpasses, railway subways, and low-lying arterial bottlenecks (e.g., Minto Bridge & ITO in Delhi, Hindmata & Milan Subway in Mumbai, Silk Board in Bengaluru, Velachery in Chennai).

📜
Historical Inundation Archive (2000–2024)

Multi-decadal flood inundation history, peak water levels, and Central Water Commission (CWC) INDOFLOODS (2000–2020) river gauge telemetry for flood risk verification.

👥
Crowdsourced Ground Truth & Reviews

Verified resident reports and community field observations, capturing local drainage rating (1 to 5) and real-world road passability during active downpours.

How AURA Synthesizes Proactive Warnings

AURA combines upcoming 6-hour rainfall forecasts with topographic depression models and municipal drainage capacity to compute Time-to-Waterlogging Threshold, trigger automated Travel Sirens, and recommend immediate traffic detour routes.
